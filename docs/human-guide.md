# SIJI Human Guide

SIJI is designed for researchers who want to move through an industry as connected objects instead of collecting isolated search results.

## Broad research

1. Open the industry overview.
2. Pick relevant product layers, positions, companies, or recent changes.
3. Follow graph relationships and paths.
4. Read the facts attached to the selected objects.
5. Open the evidence and original-source references.

## Known-company or known-product research

Search the object directly, open its stable identity, then follow its relationships, facts, and evidence.

## What to pay attention to

Each result carries useful context such as:

- relationship type;
- commercial stage;
- evidence status;
- time and scope;
- source reference;
- coverage and unknowns.

This makes it possible to keep a long research thread anchored to the same objects while adding fresh Web evidence when needed.
