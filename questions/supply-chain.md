# Supply-Chain Research Questions

Use SIJI to move between products, industry positions, companies, and typed relationships.

Typical questions:

- What sits upstream or downstream of this product?
- Which alternative technical routes exist?
- Which companies participate at a specific position?
- Which direct company relationships are published?
- Which products connect two industry positions?
- What evidence and source references support each relationship?

Start with `get_world_overview` for a broad map, or `search_entities` for a known product/company, then continue with `expand_graph`, `trace_paths`, `get_facts`, and `get_evidence`.
