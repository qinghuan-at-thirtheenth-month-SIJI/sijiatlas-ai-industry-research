# Research Case — AI Rack Upgrade and Upstream Products

## Research question

**Which upstream product layers become relevant when rack-scale AI systems change power, memory, packaging, networking, and cooling requirements?**

## Research path

```text
rack-scale platform
→ product requirements
→ upstream product layers
→ participating companies
→ commercialization facts
→ evidence and sources
```

## Public starting points

NVIDIA describes GB300 NVL72 as a rack-scale, liquid-cooled AI system.

Source:
<https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/gb300-nvl72/>

Micron disclosed HBM4 36GB 12H and 192GB SOCAMM2 production for NVIDIA Vera Rubin in March 2026.

Source:
<https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-in-High-Volume-Production-of-HBM4-Designed-for-NVIDIA-Vera-Rubin-PCIe-Gen6-SSD-and-SOCAMM2-03-16-2026/default.aspx>

TSMC publishes CoWoS-L packaging information and production milestones.

Sources:
- <https://3dfabric.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/cowos.htm>
- <https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2024/english/index.html>

## What to explore next

- platform-specific memory configurations;
- packaging generations and capacity;
- power-distribution changes;
- cooling architecture;
- interconnect and networking products;
- supplier qualification and production milestones;
- deployment and shipment evidence.

SIJI is useful here because each product generation, company, industry position, and source remains a separate research object that can be reopened later.
